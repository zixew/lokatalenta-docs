# REC-201 — Psychometric Assessment Validity Rubric & Candidate Feedback Loop

## Objective
Establish deterministic scoring guidelines, bias-mitigation protocols, and automated real-time feedback generation for candidates completing the situational judgment assessment.

## Five-Axis Competency Model
1. **System Thinking & Problem Decomposition (Weight: 25%)**
2. **Collaborative Communication & Feedback Hygiene (Weight: 20%)**
3. **Execution Resilience & Deadline Ownership (Weight: 20%)**
4. **Architectural Elegance & Anti-Slop Discipline (Weight: 20%)**
5. **Continuous Learning & Adaptive Agility (Weight: 15%)**

## Candidate Real-Time Feedback Loop
- **Instant Debrief:** Within 1,500ms of assessment submission, candidates receive a non-punitive diagnostic breakdown.
- **Developmental Pathways:** Automated pairing with LMS micro-modules targeting growth areas.
- **Privacy Assurance:** Raw psychometric telemetry is encrypted at rest via AES-256-GCM (`FIX-201`).
